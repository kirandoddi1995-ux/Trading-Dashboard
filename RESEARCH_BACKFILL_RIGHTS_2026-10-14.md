## The 15:40 close is segment- and date-specific

The [official NSE market timings page](https://www.nseindia.com/static/market-data/market-timings), retrieved 2026-10-13, lists equity derivatives normal trading at 09:15-15:40 and separately lists cash normal trading ending at 15:30 and a cash closing session starting at 15:40. Its displayed update date is 04/08/2025; this mixed page is not sufficient to assign one universal historical close to all instruments.

The more specific [official Closing Auction Session page](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/closing-auction-session), retrieved 2026-10-13, describes phased cash CAS applicability, a 15:15-15:35 CAS, a 15:50-16:00 post-close session, non-CAS securities continuously trading to 15:30, and the equity derivatives segment to 15:40. It lists circulars NSE/CMTR/72394 (2026-01-19), NSE/CMTR/73362 (2026-03-18), NSE/CMTR/74466 and NSE/FAOP/74467 (2026-05-29). The [official March SOP](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/CMTR73362.pdf) concerns cash stocks with derivatives and reference-price rules. These are separate cash/derivatives mechanisms, not interchangeable index and futures candles.

The May trading-modality PDFs were not accessible through the research tool in this pass; no exact implementation date for that derivatives timing change is certified here. Obtain the authoritative circular bytes and session calendar before assigning a historical regime. Current website timing is not proof for every 2022-2024 or later historical session. Special sessions, expiry and exchange exceptions also need their own dated rules.

For this project, treat NIFTY index values as underlying/reference evidence. A 15:40 F&O close does not establish an executable 15:40 index price, a particular futures/option bid/ask, fill availability, continuous index dissemination, the official index close publication timestamp, or the appropriate last index feature bar. Preserve separate index and F&O close windows and receipt timestamps. Do not forward-fill a missing index series through 15:40, move a later official close into an earlier feature prefix, or equate a spot/index return with derivative P&L. This distinction is an engineering inference from the segment-specific sources, not a claim of newly admitted trade data.

## Four permitted assistant uses

Signals: quote input/label/risk evidence and explain why a signal is blocked, without creating a forecast or changing a gate. Backtests: quote admitted summaries with their caveats, without filling missing performance or treating generated fixtures as market results. Capture health QA: discuss capture/storage operational receipts, missing inputs, replay and provenance; no medical interpretation and no automatic private-record reads. Report drafts: assemble fixed sections plus exact validated source quotes for human review, preserving insufficiency and licence blocks.

Any numerical fact in a supplied record is only an attributed exact quotation. It is not independently verified merely because its record hash matches. Freeform questions and stored text are untrusted, model output cannot introduce numbers, and an empty real dataset yields `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`. This delivery changes no live capture, health, package, progress, replay or trading module.
