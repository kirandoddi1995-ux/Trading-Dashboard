# Evidence readiness and model-promotion operating contract

## What is automatic

1. The scheduled collector continues to write prospective evidence to the durable PostgreSQL evidence store.
2. Real outcomes may be appended only after their configured horizon has elapsed. No historical decision is reconstructed and no outcome is synthesized.
3. `Daily model evidence readiness` runs at 17:00 Asia/Kolkata every weekday. It reads the restricted production database and emits a retained, read-only progress report.
4. The same job runs the equity candidate pipeline against the immutable individual `DECISION_EVALUATED` plus `OUTCOME_MATURED` spine.
5. `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` and `NEGATIVE` are successful fail-closed evaluations. `UNAVAILABLE` and `INVALID_EVIDENCE` fail the job and require investigation.
6. Once the basic dataset policy passes, logistic, GAM and monotonic boosted candidates are evaluated with purged walk-forward predictions, a separate chronological calibration window and an untouched holdout. The trial and its result, including negative results, are written to the experiment ledger.
7. A `VALIDATED` result still has `promotable:false` and creates no live artifact. Validation is evidence for a later governance decision, not permission to trade.

The current production count policy is stricter than the earlier monitoring milestone: at least 1,000 eligible matured observations, 100 observations in each outcome class, 120 distinct observation days, 500 nested out-of-fold observations, 500 untouched holdout observations and 20 holdout dates. Count sufficiency does not waive calibration, regime, cost, ledger or executable-quote checks.

## What is intentionally or externally manual

- **Managed artifact signing is external and currently unconfigured.** A validated in-memory bundle cannot become a production SHADOW artifact without a real managed signing key, independently supplied promotion evidence and proof of a rollback champion. The repository keeps `REQUIRE_MANAGED_PROMOTION_KEYS=true`.
- **The model registry is not connected to a durable production endpoint.** The implemented `register_shadow_candidate` API is real and signature-verifying, but its existing registry backend is local SQLite. An ephemeral GitHub runner must not pretend that local registration is durable production registration.
- **A promotion request needs two independent cryptographic approval roles.** Those signatures must be created by two distinct approvers. They cannot be generated by the training job or default to approval.
- **The protected GitHub promotion workflow remains manually dispatched.** A reviewer must approve access to the protected `production` environment. This is an intentional separation-of-duties checkpoint.
- **Actual Streamlit deployment/rollback remains provider-side.** The workflow verifies and packages an authorized release; it is not connected to the hosting provider's deployment API.

Therefore, the automatic path currently ends at a durable `VALIDATED` or fail-closed experiment result and a retained CI readiness report. The path from that result to signed SHADOW registration and a two-approver promotion request is not zero-touch and must not be described as such.

## Evidence counted by the tracker

The Streamlit **Research → Evidence Readiness** page is read-only. It counts only immutable, individual, version-matched decisions with matured outcomes. A row is training-eligible only when it has:

- available point-in-time feature values and the contract's required features;
- verified point-in-time universe membership;
- passing feature quality;
- a complete transaction-cost breakdown;
- an available executable bid and ask;
- a finite realized return; and
- timezone-aware decision/outcome timestamps in forward chronological order.

Batch Stage-1 scan summaries are not counted as training observations. The present scheduled Stage-1 path marks candidates as not having passed Stage 2 and does not append the individual complete decision/outcome contract required by the trainer. Treating those batches as completed training evidence would inflate progress and violate the point-in-time contract.

Options, futures, MCX and SMC are displayed independently. Their counts cannot reuse the equity contract or calibrator. Until each asset class has its own complete decision/outcome contract and independent training/calibration pipeline, its status remains `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` and `promotable:false`.

## Calendar-date estimate

The tracker publishes a date only after at least five distinct eligible evidence days exist. It uses the observed eligible rows per elapsed weekday and labels the result as a projection; exchange holidays, outcome-class balance, regime coverage and model-quality failures can move it later. It never estimates missing counts.

If the production spine has no eligible matured observations, the current eligible collection pace is zero and there is no finite threshold date. If the database cannot be read, the page displays `UNAVAILABLE` rather than falsely displaying zero.
