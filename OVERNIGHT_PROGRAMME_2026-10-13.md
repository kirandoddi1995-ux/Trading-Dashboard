### Acceptance-led continuation roadmap

1. **Custody first, in parallel with capacity delivery.** Owner saves key versions,
   rehearses encrypted external-disk recovery, measures Drive quota/retention and
   verifies a fresh full-image generation binding both six-table and image
   witnesses. Completion evidence is authenticated readback plus a successful
   restore into a new private target, not a checkbox or a local saved receipt.
   Watcher commissioning needs a separately controlled host and visible notice /
   dead-man failure test. Do not enable new schedules as an upload side effect.
2. **Close actual factory inventory.** Pin reviewed main sources and owner-supplied
   read-only schema/role/dependency inventory. Port multi-transaction, lease,
   original-receipt and reclamation methods explicitly; do not wrap arbitrary
   connect callbacks. Prove each restricted factory on native Postgres, including
   takeover, root CAS, append/seal, unknown COMMIT and cached factory restart.
   Derive maximum-input allocation bounds; synthetic declared bounds are not enough.
3. **Prove real historical-reader parity before switching.** Restore exact original
   rows/signatures at one trusted catalog cut, compare each business reader's
   ordering/filter/availability results against hot originals, and reject missing
   cold pages, stale roots and mixed generations. Add bounded streaming rather
   than raising an in-memory cap to fit a large archive. Finish full-image recovery
   (including database schema/roles outside the six local journal tables) separately.
4. **Commission universe shadow only after parity.** The new exact-row projection
   is now buildable. Next add source-specific restricted native schema/backfill
   rehearsal, reference reachability and root publication, preserve every header/
   snapshot and original, and measure actual allocation at transient peak. No
   claim that dedup wire savings automatically reduce pg_database_size.
5. **Source-by-source retention, then steady state.** Begin with reviewed non-ledger
   sources only after all reader/dependency coverage exists. Derive hot windows
   from real consumers and permitted retention terms, not one arbitrary age. Seal
   ledger segments only with original-key/continuation/root proofs. Archive exact
   bytes → independent custody/restore → same-cut locked retirement review. Measure
   quota and physical disk separately over repeated writer/archive cycles. Owner
   SQL/deletion remains a later explicit action, never supplied by a passed plan.
6. **Research admission and paper baseline.** Obtain source-specific usage rights,
   verified prospective quote/contract/outcome adapters and dated fee/margin/expiry
   references. Freeze the study and whole-session split before observations; retain
   every failed/insufficient trial and missing opportunity. First compare a simple
   frozen paper baseline, then ML only with enough independent sessions. Build a
   full execution simulator only after actual spread/depth/contract evidence exists.
   No unsealing 2025/2026, auto-retuning or trust from a few days of index bars.
