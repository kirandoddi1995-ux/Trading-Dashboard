## Choices and reasons

- A few correlated capture sessions cannot establish skill. Keep complete session
  blocks in walk-forward validation; train transforms only on training blocks;
  preserve point-in-time receipt versions and label availability. CPCV is not a
  substitute for a prospective frozen study and is not falsely claimed by a
  chronological splitter. Account for all trials, including failures.
- Index candles cannot establish NIFTY futures or options fills. Dated lot,
  expiry, spread/depth, broker margin and tariff evidence are indispensable.
  Unsupported partial fills and intrabar stop paths stay unsupported.
- Default LLM is a deterministic cited-evidence fallback. No new key/account,
  model download or backend is authorized; local model use later requires licence,
  RAM/CPU and latency acceptance away from capture hours. No free-credit dependence.
- PC workload competition is a safety dependency, not just speed. New work must
  stay inactive by default and bounded; real fitting and scheduling remain off.
- Release-file fingerprints must include the new optional modules. Source uses
  reviewed_stage/overnight_workbench, never held root health/packager drafts.
- Supabase's quota is database allocation, not overall disk capacity. Free projects
  become read-only above 500 MB. No cost assumption is changed. Source: [Supabase
  database and disk size](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/database-size).
